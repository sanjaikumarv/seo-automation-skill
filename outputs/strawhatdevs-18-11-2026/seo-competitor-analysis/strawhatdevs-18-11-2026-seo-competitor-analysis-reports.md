## 3. Competitor Research Methodology

- **Market and date** 18 November 2026 (IST). The sample used English search phrases with Coimbatore, India or Tamil qualifiers as appropriate. The sample provides directional evidence for these markets. Location-specific Google ranking, ads, map packs, related searches and People Also Ask were unavailable.
- **Discovery** Ran 12 query groups independently and retained up to six returned results per query. Selected competitors by geographic and commercial fit, recurring topic overlap, direct assessment of reference pages, and coverage of buyer or learner intent. Authorities, broad directories, unrelated news and government documents stayed as context where relevant.
- **Search access** The direct Google India-parameter request returned a Google redirect/interstitial page with zero result headings. The previous workflow’s browser checks had also encountered an automated-traffic challenge. The table records public web-search observations from the fallback service. Its order is not asserted as Google position. [Google query tested](https://www.google.com/search?q=software+development+company+in+Coimbatore&gl=in&hl=en&pws=0)
- **Source review** Screened robots.txt and inspected selected source HTML with Requests and Beautiful Soup at low concurrency. Checked representative journeys with Chrome/Playwright. Collected first advertised sitemap XML per site and retained structure/counts. Reviewed direct referring source anchors separately. Screaming Frog CLI was unavailable on PATH.
- **Evidence labels** **VERIFIED** denotes a directly measured response, parsed tag or verified anchor. **OBSERVED** covers public page content, competitor claims and fallback search entries. **ESTIMATED** is reserved for a labeled vendor estimate. **INFERRED** denotes the analytical relationship or recommendation. **UNAVAILABLE** denotes data the research could not verify.
- **Metric boundary** Priorities reflect documented offer fit, expected buyer intent, existing page suitability, visible competitor specificity, implementation effort and conversion path. Search volume, KD, CPC, traffic, actual conversions and revenue remain NOT AVAILABLE. Coverage and observed repeat appearances are counts within this defined sample.
