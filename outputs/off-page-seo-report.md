## Three original-asset campaign concepts

### 1. A practical workflow handoff benchmark for SMEs

Research question: where do manufacturing/dealer teams lose visibility between order acceptance and fulfillment? Create a small consented study or a clearly labelled synthetic demonstration, publish the questionnaire, anonymization approach and limitations, and show workflow diagrams. **There are no collected results yet.** Do not invent “hours saved” or a client case study. Initial useful asset: a requirements checklist and an order-handoff map based on the existing solution page. Audience: relevant local business communities and software-practitioner publications; verify editorial relevance and current participation rules before approaching anyone.

### 2. A transparent Next.js canonical and locale test kit

Publish an open-source, read-only checklist/test harness for self-canonicals, locale-equivalent hreflang, sitemap redirects and invalid-locale routing. Use a demo fixture or the owner's approved site evidence, not public accusations against competitors. Include reproducible methods and expected error cases. Distribution candidates: relevant project documentation, developer communities and maintainers who accept such resources. No guarantee of editorial inclusion or backlinks.

### 3. From a learning project to a business-ready e-commerce plan

Use the existing CartCraft educational theme to publish a separate store-launch requirements guide: payments, inventory, fulfillment, security responsibilities, migrations and customer support. Clearly distinguish a course exercise from a client production implementation. Pair it with the proposed e-commerce service page only after that page is live and useful. Invite genuine expert feedback; do not disguise paid placements as independent recommendations.

These concepts use actual site themes and user offerings. No campaigns have launched, no surveys have run, and no publication commitments exist. Journalism-request services and editorial communities change; verify availability at execution time instead of relying on legacy platform names in the source skill.
