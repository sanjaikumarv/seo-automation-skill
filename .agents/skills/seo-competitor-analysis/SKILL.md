# SEO Competitor Analysis & Competitive Intelligence

## Skill Description

An advanced SEO competitive intelligence and market benchmarking skill:
- **What this skill does:** Identifies direct business and SERP competitors, reverse-engineers competitor website architecture and landing pages, analyzes keyword overlaps and content gaps, audits competitor technical and schema implementation, evaluates backlink profiles from public observational data, and prioritizes actionable opportunities.
- **Its primary objective:** Pinpoint competitive advantages and market gaps to outperform domain competitors in search visibility without fabricating unverified metrics.
- **When it should be used:** Stage 3 of the SEO workflow, immediately following the completion of `seo-keyword-research`.
- **What output or result it produces:** Generates `outputs/{{CURRENT_DATE}}/{{DOMAIN_NAME}}/competitor-analysis/Competitor_Analysis_Report.md` and supporting gap datasets.

## 1. ROLE & RESPONSIBILITY

You are a Senior SEO Competitor Analyst, Technical SEO Specialist, Competitive Intelligence Researcher, Keyword Gap Analyst, Content Gap Analyst, Backlink Research Analyst, and SEO Automation Engineer.

Your responsibility is to execute a complete, evidence-based, end-to-end SEO Competitor Analysis for the provided website.

1. SEO Audit.
2. Keyword Research & Keyword Mapping.

## STAGE 6: COMPETITOR KEYWORD GAP ANALYSIS

Compare the target website's keyword coverage against competitor keyword coverage.

Use:

- Keyword_Research_Data.csv.
- Keyword_Clusters.csv.
- Keyword_Mapping.csv.
- Keyword_Gap_Analysis.csv.
- Competitor_Keyword_Analysis.csv.
- Competitor_Page_Analysis.csv.

Identify:

### A. Shared Keywords

Keywords with relevant evidence for both the target website and competitors.

### B. Missing Keywords

Competitors have relevant coverage, but the target website lacks a suitable page or meaningful content coverage.

### C. Weak Coverage Keywords

The target website has a related page or topic, but its content or search intent coverage may be insufficient.

### D. Unique Target Website Keywords

Relevant keyword topics where the target website has content or positioning not observed in the selected competitors.

### E. New Opportunities

Relevant keyword topics discovered during competitor analysis that were not included in the original Keyword Research dataset.

For every gap:

- Keyword.
- Keyword cluster.
- Search intent.
- Competitor domain.
- Competitor URL.
- Target website URL, if available.
- Gap type.
- Available search metrics.
- Evidence source.
- Recommended action.

Do not claim a competitor ranks for a keyword without ranking evidence.

Save:

`Competitor_Keyword_Gap.csv`
