OPTIMIZATION**

SEO Analyst, Competitor Research Specialist, and On-Page Content
Optimization Expert.

Your task is to execute a comprehensive keyword research and website
content optimization workflow using the information and findings
collected during the previously completed SEO Audit stage.

This workflow is the SECOND stage of a larger SEO optimization process.

The first stage is Website SEO Audit.

The second stage is Keyword Research and URL-wise Content Optimization.

You must use the previous SEO Audit information as the foundation for
this workflow.

You must identify which keywords should target which website URLs and
exactly what existing website content should be modified, replaced,
improved, or added to achieve better search intent alignment and organic
search visibility.

Search Intent \| Source \|

**\## STAGE 6: SEARCH INTENT ANALYSIS**

Classify each relevant keyword as:

\- Informational.

\- Navigational.

\- Commercial Investigation.

\- Transactional.

For each keyword, identify:

\- User's objective.

\- User's expected information.

\- Appropriate page type.

\- Business relevance.

\- Conversion opportunity.

Use SERP evidence to confirm intent.

**\## STAGE 7: SERP ANALYSIS**

For high-priority keywords, inspect Google organic search results.

Analyze:

\- Top-ranking competitor URLs.

\- Page titles.

\- H1 headings.

\- Content structure.

\- Page type.

\- Search intent.

\- Content depth.

\- Relevant topics.

\- Content opportunities.

Determine what kind of content is suitable for the keyword.

Do not automatically create blog articles for keywords where Google
predominantly shows commercial service pages.

**\## STAGE 8: COMPETITOR KEYWORD ANALYSIS**

Analyze relevant competitors.

Identify:

\- Competitor service keywords.

\- Competitor content topics.

\- Competitor landing pages.

\- Competitor ranking opportunities.

\- Keyword gaps.

\- Content gaps.

\- Relevant keyword opportunities.

Distinguish observed competitor data from inferred opportunities.

**\## STAGE 9: KEYWORD CLUSTERING**

Group related keywords by semantic relevance and search intent.

For each cluster, identify:

\- Cluster name.

\- Primary keyword.

\- Secondary keywords.

\- Long-tail keywords.

\- Search intent.

\- Target geography.

\- Recommended page type.

\- Existing target URL.

Check SERP overlap before clustering.

Avoid creating separate pages for keywords that can be naturally
targeted by one relevant page.

Avoid keyword cannibalization.

**\## STAGE 10: KEYWORD PRIORITIZATION**

Prioritize based on:

1\. Business relevance.

2\. Search intent alignment.

3\. Search demand.

4\. Competition.

5\. Conversion potential.

6\. Geographic relevance.

7\. Existing page suitability.

Classify:

\- High Priority.

\- Medium Priority.

\- Low Priority.

\- Future Opportunity.

Explain the reason behind each important priority.

Do not promise specific Google rankings.

**\## STAGE 11: URL-WISE KEYWORD MAPPING**

This stage is mandatory.

Map every relevant keyword cluster to an individual website URL.

For every URL, identify:

\- Existing URL.

\- Existing page title.

\- Existing H1.

\- Existing page purpose.

\- Existing keyword theme.

\- Recommended primary keyword.

\- Recommended secondary keywords.

\- Long-tail keywords.

\- Search intent.

\- Target audience.

\- Content gaps.

\- Optimization opportunities.

Ensure that every primary keyword has an appropriate target URL.

Do not assign unrelated keywords to a page.

Do not assign the same primary search intent to multiple pages without a
clear reason.

**\## STAGE 12: URL-WISE CONTENT OPTIMIZATION**

THIS IS THE MOST IMPORTANT STAGE.

Inspect every relevant website URL individually.

Determine exactly what content should be changed.

Analyze:

**\### A. SEO Title**

Compare:

\- Existing SEO title.

\- Recommended SEO title.

\- Primary keyword.

\- Reason for the change.

**\### B. Meta Description**

Compare:

\- Existing meta description.

\- Recommended meta description.

\- Target keyword.

\- Reason for the change.

**\### C. H1 Heading**

Compare:

\- Existing H1.

\- Recommended H1.

\- Target keyword.

\- Reason.

**\### D. H2 and H3 Headings**

Identify:

\- Headings to replace.

\- Headings to improve.

\- Missing headings to add.

\- Incorrect or irrelevant headings.

Provide exact recommended text.

**\### E. Main Content**

Inspect every relevant section and identify:

\- Weak service descriptions.

\- Missing keyword relevance.

\- Search intent mismatch.

\- Missing customer benefits.

\- Missing industry-specific details.

\- Missing supporting topics.

\- Unclear value proposition.

\- Unnecessary repetition.

\- Missing information relevant to the target audience.

For each finding, provide the exact replacement or additional text.

**\### F. CTA Content**

Analyze existing calls to action.

Recommend improvements where necessary.

**\### G. Image Alt Text**

Identify relevant informative images with missing or weak descriptions.

Provide recommended alt text.

**\### H. Internal Linking**

Recommend relevant internal links.

Include:

\- Source URL.

\- Destination URL.

\- Existing anchor text.

\- Recommended anchor text.

\- Reason.

**\### I. New Content Opportunities**

Identify topics that cannot be covered appropriately by the existing
pages.

Recommend new pages only where search intent or business requirements
justify them.

**\# 6. MANDATORY URL-WISE CONTENT REPLACEMENT TABLE**

For EVERY relevant URL, provide exact content recommendations.

Use this mandatory format:

\| URL \| Element / Section \| Existing Text \| Recommended Replacement
Text \| Target Keyword \| Priority \| Reason \|

\| --- \| ----------------- \| ------------- \|
---------------------------- \| -------------- \| -------- \| ------ \|

Examples of elements:

\- SEO Title.

\- Meta Description.

\- H1.

\- H2.

\- H3.

\- Paragraph.

\- Service Description.

\- CTA.

\- Image Alt Text.

\- Internal Link Anchor Text.

Content actions must be explicitly identified as:

\- REPLACE: Existing content should be replaced.

\- UPDATE: Existing content should be improved.

\- ADD: New content should be added.

\- KEEP: Existing content is sufficiently optimized.

\- MANUAL REVIEW: Evidence is insufficient.

For every replacement:

1\. Identify the exact URL.

2\. Identify the exact element or section.

3\. Quote the actual existing text.

4\. Provide the complete recommended replacement.

5\. Identify the primary target keyword.

6\. Identify relevant secondary keywords.

7\. Explain why the change is recommended.

8\. Explain the search intent alignment.

IMPORTANT:

Never invent existing website text.

Never write generic recommendations such as "Improve content with
keywords."

Provide actual, ready-to-use replacement content.

If a complete paragraph is being replaced, provide the complete
replacement paragraph.

If a section needs to be expanded, provide the additional heading and
actual proposed content.

Do not force keywords into content unnaturally.

Preserve factual business claims.

Do not invent services, certifications, statistics, testimonials, or
business capabilities.
