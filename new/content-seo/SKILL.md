### Topic cluster linking rules

- Supporting articles should link to relevant pillar pages.
- Pillar pages should link to relevant supporting articles.
- Related articles may link to each other when contextually appropriate.
- Use natural anchor text.
- Avoid excessive exact-match anchor text.
- Avoid circular or irrelevant links.
