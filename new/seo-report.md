# Final SEO Report

## Objective

Generate a comprehensive SEO analysis report.

## Required Inputs

- WEBSITE_AUDIT_RESULT
- KEYWORD_RESEARCH_RESULT
- COMPETITOR_ANALYSIS_RESULT
- ON_PAGE_SEO_RESULT

## Instructions

1. Review all previous skill outputs.
2. Consolidate findings.
3. Identify critical SEO problems.
4. Prioritize recommendations.
5. Prepare a short-term action plan.
6. Prepare a medium-term SEO strategy.
7. Prepare a long-term SEO strategy.
8. Identify missing data and limitations.

## Output Structure

1. Executive Summary
2. Website Overview
3. Technical SEO Audit
4. Keyword Research
5. Competitor Analysis
6. On-Page SEO
7. Critical Issues
8. Recommended Fixes
9. 30-Day SEO Action Plan
10. 60-Day SEO Action Plan
11. 90-Day SEO Action Plan
12. Final Recommendations

## Rules

- Do not invent metrics.
- Preserve source evidence.
- Do not remove important findings.
- Identify unverified assumptions.
- Ensure recommendations are actionable.

## Completion

Return FINAL_SEO_REPORT.
